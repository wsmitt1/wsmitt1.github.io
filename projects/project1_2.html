Denet

A point-and-click puzzle game built as a team project at FutureGames.

Unity C# Team Project

Overview

Denet is a point-and-click game where you explore your old computer and solve puzzles to uncover the truth. I worked on many of the core systems, including the player movement system, the combat system and the many password puzzles. This game was built in Unity using C# as a team project at FutureGames. We had a team of 8 people, and I was one of the programmers on the project.

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges of this project was the scope. We had a lot of ideas for the game, and it was difficult to narrow down what we wanted to include in the final product. We had to make a lot of tough decisions about what features to cut and what to keep. Another big challenge was that the idea of the game was not fully fleshed out when we started development. We had a general idea of what we wanted to do, but a lot of the details were still up in the air. This made it difficult to plan out our development and caused a lot of changes and rework throughout the project.

What I Learned

Denet was a great learning experience for me. I learned a lot about working in a team, and how to communicate effectively with my teammates. I also learned a lot about game development in general, and how to manage a project of this scope.
